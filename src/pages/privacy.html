Privacy Policy

How personal information is handled when you contact or book with EMatolweni Guesthouse.

Privacy Policy details

Effective date: 26 August 2026
Last updated: 27 August 2026

This is a plain-language operational draft. It describes the website configuration visible in the source code and the privacy practices the guesthouse should follow. It is not legal advice, does not claim certification or full POPIA compliance, and should be checked by the guesthouse owner and a POPIA-qualified legal reviewer.

2. Information collected through the website

Contact form

The contact form asks for your first name, surname, email address, telephone number, intended check-in and check-out dates, and an optional message. It also records that the required privacy consent checkbox was selected. Please do not include identity numbers, card details, health information or other sensitive information in the message field.

Online submission is currently disabled while the connection from the planned Afrihost-hosted website to Netlify Forms is being configured and verified. This version does not send the completed form to either service. You can still use the separate contact details.

Bookings and payments

Booking links direct you to NightsBridge. NightsBridge states that its booking services store booking information for accommodation providers and may pass transaction information to a connected payment provider. The guesthouse has confirmed that it does not keep bank-account or payment-card details. The contact form does not ask for banking or payment information. The exact payment option configured for this property is not identified in the repository.

Technical information

When a browser loads this site, its host and third-party resources may receive technical details such as an IP address, browser or device information, request time, referring page and pages or files requested. The repository does not contain analytics, advertising pixels or custom cookie tracking code. Third-party embeds and services may apply their own technologies and policies.

3. Why information is collected

Personal information may be used to:

  • answer an enquiry and communicate with you;
  • understand and assist with your booking requirements;
  • create, manage and fulfil a booking when you choose to proceed;
  • process or reconcile payments through the applicable booking and payment services;
  • maintain operational, accounting and legally required records;
  • protect guests, the guesthouse and its systems, and address disputes or misuse; and
  • respond to lawful requests and exercise or defend legal rights.

The guesthouse has confirmed that enquiry information is not sold or used for unrelated direct marketing.

4. Basis for processing

The website asks for your consent before the contact form can be sent. You can withdraw that consent for future processing by contacting the guesthouse. Withdrawal does not automatically undo processing that already took place.

Depending on the circumstances, some processing may also be needed to take steps you request before a booking, to perform or administer a booking, to meet a legal obligation, or to protect a legitimate interest. This policy does not make a final legal determination about those bases; the owner and a qualified reviewer should confirm which basis applies to each activity.

6. Who information may be shared with

The owner must confirm who can access Netlify submissions and enquiry mailboxes, including any administrator, developer or forwarding recipient. These access arrangements have not been verified in this review. Information may otherwise need to be shared with:

  • website hosting, form handling, email, booking and payment service providers;
  • professional advisers, insurers or service providers where needed for legitimate operational or legal purposes; and
  • regulators, courts, law-enforcement bodies or other parties when disclosure is lawfully required or necessary to protect rights and safety.

The guesthouse has confirmed that personal information is not sold or used for unrelated direct marketing.

7. Retention and deletion

The retention period and deletion schedule for enquiries have not yet been confirmed by the owner. This includes Netlify submissions, notification emails, forwarded or exported copies, server logs and backups. No automatic deletion schedule has been implemented by this form.

Before enabling submissions, the owner should confirm how long each copy is kept, who reviews and deletes it, and what changes when an enquiry becomes a booking. Removing a submission from Netlify does not remove the separate email copies. Provider backup arrangements also need confirmation. Contact the guesthouse for its current retention arrangements.

8. Reasonable security measures

Reasonable measures should be appropriate to the sensitivity and volume of information handled. They should include limiting access to authorised people, using unique protected accounts and multi-factor authentication where supported, keeping systems updated, reviewing provider access, and disposing of records securely. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Account permissions, multi-factor authentication, backup handling and incident-response arrangements still require owner confirmation. This source-code review does not verify the security of the hosting, Netlify or email accounts.

9. Your privacy rights

Subject to POPIA and any applicable limitations, you may ask the guesthouse to:

  • confirm whether it holds personal information about you and request access to it;
  • correct or update information that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date;
  • object to certain processing;
  • request deletion or destruction where the information may no longer lawfully be kept; or
  • withdraw consent for future consent-based processing.

Send a request to info@ematolweniguesthouse.co.za. The guesthouse may need enough information to verify your identity and locate the relevant records. Some records may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it.

10. Withdrawing consent and making a complaint

To withdraw consent, object to processing or raise a privacy complaint, email the guesthouse at info@ematolweniguesthouse.co.za or call +27 (78) 592 6801. Please describe the information or activity concerned and how you would like the matter addressed.

You may also contact South Africa's Information Regulator. Its official contact page currently lists POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za for POPIA complaints and provides its complaint process and other contact channels.

11. Cross-border processing

Hosting, form, booking, payment, map, font or content-delivery providers may process or store information outside South Africa. The exact locations can vary by provider and service. The guesthouse should review the providers' current privacy and contractual terms and use services with appropriate cross-border protections where POPIA requires them.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when website services or privacy practices change. The effective and last-updated dates above should be revised whenever a material change is published.
